A proportional room layout generated from your plot size and storey count — useful for sizing rooms, not for permits or construction. Have an architect prepare working drawings before you build.

About these numbers. Naqsha uses common rule-of-thumb ratios used by South Asian contractors for early-stage budgeting (e.g. ~8.5 bricks, 0.42 cement bags, 3.5 kg steel per sq.ft of covered area). Actual quantities depend on your structural design, soil report, wall thickness and finishes. Figures exclude labour, doors & windows, electrical, plumbing, sanitary fittings, boundary wall and approvals — these typically add 35–60% on top of material cost. Material rates are editable because they change often and vary by city and supplier; treat every figure here as a planning estimate, and get quotes from a local contractor or engineer before budgeting a real project.
